Who can sell

If people do skilled, hands-on work at your site, that work is valuable training data.

Site owners

Businesses running industrial, agricultural or logistics sites. Earn a rate for every accepted hour recorded on your site.

Operating teams

Workers who wear a camera during normal shifts. They're paid directly for their time, on top of their normal wage.

Footage archives

Existing footage from line cameras or past projects, if you own it and everyone in it agreed to recording.

What data is valid

Labs pay for real, consented, usable footage. Anything else is rejected at our quality check and isn't paid.

Accepted Paid

  • Real work in industrial, agricultural or logistics settings
  • Hands-on tasks with tools, machines or materials
  • Helmet, chest, wrist or fixed cameras
  • Continuous sessions of 20 minutes or more
  • Everyone on camera has signed consent
  • Footage you own or have the right to sell

Not accepted Rejected

  • Staged or acted footage
  • Hidden cameras or recording without consent
  • Anyone under 18 on camera
  • Confidential documents, screens or customer designs in view
  • Footage taken from the internet or owned by someone else
  • Heavily edited, filtered or watermarked video

From application to your first payout, we handle the hard parts.

    Tell us about your site, the work done there and roughly how many people do it.

    We agree what's recorded, check safety with your team and collect consent from everyone on camera.

Questions sellers ask

Does it cost anything?

No. We supply and maintain the equipment, and quality checks and labelling are on us.

Will recording slow down our work?

No. Devices are fitted around the work, and your team records during normal shifts.

What about confidential processes?

You decide what's recorded. We blur screens, documents and anything else you flag before the data leaves the site.

How are our workers protected?

Everyone on camera gives individual consent in their own language, can opt out at any time, is paid for their time, and can have their face blurred.

Who owns the data?

You keep your site and your processes. You license the recorded data to Pulse, which licenses it to AI labs. The terms are set out in your agreement.

Do you only work in Africa, India, Asia and Russia?

That's where our partner network is today. Apply from anywhere and we'll tell you whether we can support your site.
