# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS in a single `index.html`, no build step. Deploy target undecided.

## Users

Two buyer groups, weighted equally:

- **Robotics and VLA / world-model labs** that need real-world egocentric and manipulation data beyond lab and kitchen footage.
- **Industrial AI and computer-vision teams** building safety, inspection, and process models who need footage and sensor data from real production floors.

Both are evaluating whether a data vendor can reach real industrial environments, deliver clean, consented, well-labelled data, and fit their training stack.

## Product Purpose

Pulse Data collects and labels physical-world data from hard-to-reach industrial sites for AI training. Success is a buyer requesting a sample or a quote.

## Positioning

Access through a network of family offices that own operating companies in remote, industrial locations, all of which already serve large clients. Because Pulse Data is that close to the sites and their owners, it can install whatever capture devices a buyer needs on site, while keeping collection ethical and the resulting data clean and ready to train on.

## Operating Context

Confirmed site types and regions in the partner network:

- Mines and foundries in Africa
- Packaging businesses in India
- Manufacturing businesses in Asia
- Farms in Russia

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Data collection on partner sites, with any type of capture device installed to the buyer's spec.
- Data labelling.
- Ethical collection (consent) and clean, training-ready data are core commitments.
- Undecided: pricing, minimum orders, delivery formats, licence terms, capacity, and site counts. Do not state these as facts.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Pulse Data.
- Reference sites the founder named for layout: physicaldata.ai (primary), linear.app, lemni.com.

## Evidence on Hand

- One underground mining frame: an egocentric view of a hand-held rock drill, hi-vis sleeves and gloves, timestamped 25/11/2026 08:42:17 (`assets/mine-drill.webp`). Provided by the founder for use as a sample dataset frame.
- No customers, testimonials, case studies, volumes, prices, or benchmarks yet. Do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Access is the product: show the sites, not abstractions about data.
2. Never claim numbers or customers that do not exist yet.
3. Ethics and data cleanliness are stated plainly and specifically.
4. Speak to labs and industrial teams in their own terms.
